# Asset credits

All photo textures and scanned models in this folder come from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) and are
released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). The glTF models were decimated and repacked with Blender for the web.
Leaf and twig atlases were cut from the Poly Haven tree scans listed below and recomposed at runtime into
crown cards.

## Textures (tex/)

Terrain layers: mud_forest, forrest_ground_01, dirt_floor, aerial_rocks_02, gravel_ground_01, snow_02,
dry_ground_01, brown_mud_leaves_01, forest_leaves_02, aerial_rocks_04, rocky_terrain_02, rocky_terrain_03.

Bark: bark_brown_02, knotted_pine_bark, bark_willow, japanese_camphor_bark, eucalyptus_bark,
pine_tree_01 (bark), jacaranda_tree (trunk), island_tree_01 (trunk).

Rock: sandstone_cracks (Pride Rock), rock_pitted_mossy.

Foliage atlases: island_tree_01 (leaves), tree_small_02 (leaves), jacaranda_tree (leaves),
pine_tree_01 (twigs), grass_medium_02 (blades, fresh and dry), fern_02, moss_01, shrub_03.
